Architecture tour

The whole system as one read, before any question: the units in the order to learn them, each after the units it uses.

Learning path

    Minutes are a rough budget: 1 to read an orientation and 4 for a walkthrough on first contact, 2 per ladder level 1-3 still to pass, 1 per review due, 3 for a re-check.

    Quiz

    Each unit at your level on the question ladder, one fact per question. At levels 1 and 2 a miss shows the answer; it counts as the miss it was, and comes back in review.

    Review

    The questions you missed come back at growing intervals (1 day, 3 days, then longer), in other words.

    Tutor

    A conversation about one unit: the tutor reads the code for you and asks back. Say "check me" (or keep going a few turns) for a check question at your level; it is graded and recorded.

    Ten minutes: a good place to stop. What you answered is recorded; the path says what is next.

    Teach-back

    Explain a unit in your own words: what it is for, the decision behind it and what was not chosen. You hear what was right, what was missing and any misconception; it counts as level 2.
